# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss decisions and code directly, give useful context, accept good-faith corrections and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or pull requests to share credentials or private conversations.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, restrict participation or remove a contributor from project spaces. They should explain decisions where appropriate and handle reports privately, with attention to the affected people.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact a maintainer has explicitly made available on their repository profile. Do not post personal details in public issues. If no safe private channel is available, use the hosting platform's reporting tools. Maintainers must establish and document a private reporting channel before opening additional community spaces.

This original project policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions and other spaces managed by the project.
