# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Agent Islands should be a welcoming place to collaborate, regardless of experience, background, identity or disability. Treat contributors and users with respect and make room for people who are learning.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the code, evidence and decisions; do not attack the person.
- Give constructive feedback, useful reproduction details and time for others to respond.
- Accept good-faith corrections and acknowledge the impact of a mistake.
- Respect different perspectives, communication styles and levels of experience.
- Protect private information, including prompts, credentials, account details and screenshots.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information, impersonate people, retaliate against a report or repeatedly target a contributor after being asked to stop.

Good-faith disagreement about a technical or product decision is welcome. Using disagreement as a reason for abuse is not.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other spaces maintained by the project. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project in another community. Participation does not make a contributor an official representative.

## Reporting

For harmful public content, use GitHub's **Report content** tools. For a private report to a maintainer, use a private contact they have explicitly made available on their profile. Do not include personal details in public issues. If no safe private contact is available, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines#reporting-abuse).

Include a link to the relevant content, what happened and any immediate safety concern. Share only information necessary to assess the report. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Maintainer response

Maintainers review reports in context and should protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical. They may request a correction, issue a warning, remove harmful content, restrict participation temporarily or exclude a person from project spaces. Severe threats or disclosure of private information may warrant immediate restrictions.

Explain decisions to affected people when it is safe and appropriate. A person involved in a report should not be its sole decision-maker; use another uninvolved maintainer when available, or the hosting platform's reporting process. Appeals can use the same safe private channel and should include new context.

No response-time guarantee is implied. This is an original project policy, covered by the repository's MIT License.
